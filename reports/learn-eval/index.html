Learned handwriting model: evaluation

Six synthetic writers with known variation, plus your own handwriting. For each letter the model trains on 20 copies (half of yours) and is tested against copies it has never seen. "Previous method" is the old approach (random tilt, size, wobble and pressure applied to real copies). Generated 11/11/2026, 3:17:26 PM.

1-NN two-sample testMix generated copies with unseen real ones; for each, is its nearest neighbour the same kind? 50% = indistinguishable, 100% = obviously different. Real-vs-real shown for reference.
NoveltyHow far generated copies sit from the training copies, relative to how far genuinely new real copies sit. 1.0 = as new as a real copy, below = copying, far above = off-style.
Feature offset / spread errorWidth, height, slant and ink weight vs the unseen real copies: mean offset in standard deviations (0 ideal) and log spread ratio (0 = same variability).
Unseen explainedHow much of the variation of copies the model never saw its patterns can express. Ground-truth recovery: correlation between a learned pattern and a true cause of variation (synthetic writers only).
